The best radio stations in the world

Updated October 2026 · 7 min read

There are more than forty thousand radio stations streaming online right now, and almost all of them are free.

For discovering music you have never heard

BBC Radio 6 Music

Probably the single best argument that curated radio still beats an algorithm.

NTS Radio

Broadcasting from London, Manchester, Shanghai and Los Angeles.

Radio Nova (Paris)

Nova built its identity on the idea that genre borders are arbitrary.

For news you can trust

News radio is where the medium still has a clear advantage.

  • BBC World Service
  • France Info
  • NPR
  • Monte Carlo Doualiya

For the Arab world

Arabic-language radio online is richer than most international lists admit.

For background and focus

Not every listening session needs a presenter.

How to find your own, in three minutes

  1. Start with a country, not a genre.
  2. Give each station four minutes.
  3. Save anything you liked even slightly.

Why radio still works

Streaming services solved the problem of access — any song, any time.

The best station is not the one with the biggest audience.
