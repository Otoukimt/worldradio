Pope Leo XIV in Peru: Andes Flight and Amazon Defense

Pope Leo XIV is conducting a historic visit to Peru on Sunday 15 November 2026, traveling between the high summits of the Andes and the dense forests of the Peruvian Amazon. As reported by Il Fatto Quotidiano and RaiNews, the Pontiff flew across the Andes aboard a helicopter bound for Santa Cruz. During this journey through the skies of Peru, he shared moments of genuine warmth, simplicity, and smiles inside the cockpit alongside the military pilots of the Peruvian Air Force. The journey highlights the human side of the papacy, breaking protocol to connect directly with service members while flying over difficult terrain to reach remote communities.

The trip combines major spiritual gatherings with visits to remote indigenous communities. Along his route, Pope Leo XIV experienced local devotions and warm receptions from native groups, underscoring the spiritual and social themes of his South American pilgrimage. The flight across the high Andes serves as a symbol of bridge-building between isolated regions and the global Church, emphasizing that geographically distant populations remain at the core of the Vatican's pastoral attention.

Angelus in Cusco and Messages on Illness

In the imperial city of Cusco, an area rich in popular religious traditions, Pope Leo XIV led the Angelus prayer on Sunday 15 November 2026. As reported by Sky TG24, the Pope addressed gathered crowds, stating that the world often discards the misfortune of illness. Vatican News highlighted his message that illness does not diminish a person, but rather multiplies the talents of love. This powerful call challenges modern societal views on suffering, urging communities to value human dignity regardless of physical or health conditions.

The gatherings in Cusco brought together large crowds of faithful celebrating centuries-old local devotions. As noted by Avvenire, the presence of the Pope among these crowds reflects broader reflections on traditional faith, regional devotions, and modern societal changes. The interaction between centuries-old Andean customs and papal teaching shows how ancient traditions continue to inform contemporary ethical debates on human vulnerability and solidarity.

Mission to the Amazon and Protection of Indigenous Lands

According to Euronews, Pope Leo XIV arrived in the Peruvian Amazon on Sunday 15 November 2026 to advocate for the defense of indigenous peoples and the protection of the rainforest. His presence in the region highlights ongoing environmental concerns and the rights of native tribes. By stepping directly into the forest, the Pope reinforces the moral imperative to protect delicate ecosystems from overexploitation while ensuring native populations retain control over their ancestral territories.

During his stay in the indigenous community, unique scenes of local wildlife were observed. Corriere della Sera described moments within the welcoming native village where a sloth and a small monkey played together alongside other community animals during the papal reception. This harmonious picture of humans, wildlife, and nature reflected the integral ecology that the Pontiff seeks to promote throughout his South American journey.
