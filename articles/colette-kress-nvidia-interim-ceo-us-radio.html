Updated 2026-11-13

On Friday 13 November 2026, Nvidia announced a major leadership transition as longtime chief financial officer Colette Kress stepped in as interim chief executive officer. The change follows the resignation of Jensen Huang amid affair scandals, marking a dramatic turning point for one of the world's most valuable technology companies. As financial markets and listeners tuned in to updates across business news programs, the sudden shift placed a familiar internal figure at the helm during an unexpected corporate crisis.

The departure of Huang leaves a massive vacuum at the chipmaker, which has driven the global artificial intelligence boom. Kress, a graduate of SMU's Cox School of Business, has spent years managing Nvidia's financial strategy and public communications, often acting as a steady voice during periods of intense market scrutiny. According to reports from Reuters and Yahoo Finance, the board of directors moved quickly to appoint her, aiming to reassure investors and maintain operational stability.
