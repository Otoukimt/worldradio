Nico de Boinville Escorted Off Track After Security Threat

Updated 2026-11-17
▶ Listen to talkSPORT now

British horse racing faced a serious security incident on Tuesday 17 November 2026 when top jockey Nico de Boinville was given security protection and escorted off the track at Plumpton. The measure was taken after a severe threat was made against him following losses on two race favorites. According to reports from the Racing Post and talkSPORT, security personnel intervened directly to guarantee the rider's safety.

Reports from The Independent and The Mirror noted that de Boinville was escorted out of the weighing room area as security officials acted swiftly to handle the situation. The incident highlighted growing concerns over athlete protection at sporting venues when sudden hostility arises from disappointed spectators or outside threats.

Security Escort at Plumpton Racetrack

The safety response was triggered at Plumpton after details of the threat emerged during the race meeting. As reported by the Daily Mail and The Irish Sun, extra security staff surrounded Nico de Boinville to prevent any potential harm and remove him safely from the premises.

Officials on-site acted rapidly to assess the threat level and clear a safe passage for the jockey. Racing authorities monitor such incidents closely to determine whether additional safety measures are required across future race meetings in the United Kingdom.
