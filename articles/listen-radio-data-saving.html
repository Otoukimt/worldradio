Tips

Listen to radio without burning your data

Updated October 2026 · 6 min read

Internet radio is cheaper on data than almost anything else you stream.

What an hour of radio really costs

Stream quality1 hour3 h/day, 30 days
48 kbps~22 MB~1.9 GB
64 kbps~29 MB~2.6 GB
128 kbps~58 MB~5.2 GB
320 kbps~144 MB~13 GB

Halving the bitrate halves the data.

1. Pick the low-bitrate version

Large stations publish several streams of the same programme.

2. Prefer AAC over MP3

At the same bitrate, AAC sounds clearly better than MP3.

3. Talk and news do not need music quality

The human voice occupies a narrow frequency range.

4. Close the tab, do not just lock the screen

A stream keeps downloading as long as it is playing.

5. Use a sleep timer at night

A sleep timer is the single most effective data-saving setting.

6. Set a data warning

Both Android and iOS can show per-app data use.

7. Favourite your stations

Every station you sample loads a few seconds of audio.

8. Use Wi-Fi for the long sessions

The hours you spend at home or at work are the bulk of your listening.

9. Watch out for stations that reconnect

An unstable station re-buffers each time.

The short version

  • 64 kbps AAC for music on mobile.
  • Stop the stream instead of locking the screen.
  • Sleep timer every night.
  • Long sessions on Wi-Fi.
