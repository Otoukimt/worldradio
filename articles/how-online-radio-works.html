How online radio streaming works

Updated October 2026 · 8 min read

When you tap play on an internet radio station, nothing is downloaded.

The stream URL

Every station has a stream URL.

Codec: how the audio is packed

Raw audio is far too large to send continuously.

  • MP3
  • AAC and AAC+
  • Opus and Ogg Vorbis

Bitrate: how much data per second

Bitrate is measured in kilobits per second.

BitrateSounds likeData per hour
32–48 kbpsSpeech, AM-like~15–22 MB
64 kbps AACGood for talk~29 MB
128 kbps MP3Standard music quality~58 MB
320 kbpsNear CD~144 MB

Buffering: why there is a delay

Your player never plays audio the instant it arrives.

Internet radio is always a few seconds behind.

Why some stations will not play in a browser

A secure website is not allowed to load audio from an insecure address.

Metadata: the track name that appears

Alongside the audio, many stations send small text updates.

What this means in practice

  • If a station stutters, the problem is usually your connection.
  • Compare bitrates before blaming your headphones.
  • A station that never starts is almost always blocked or offline.
