What the reports say about Jensen Huang and his final days

The timeline pieced together on 13 November 2026 paints a turbulent picture behind the scenes at one of the world's most influential technology firms. Reuters reported on 12 November 2026 that sources pointed to a relationship involving a second Nvidia employee. By Friday morning, Mashable noted that The Daily Show host Desi Lydic was already reacting on air to reports that Huang allegedly had three affairs, highlighting how rapidly the corporate scandal crossed into mainstream comedy and public debate.

Other reporting focused on Huang's demeanor inside the company just before his departure. According to Fortune, accounts from his final days described a broken executive heard saying phrases like "whatever" and "I don't care" as pressure mounted. Meanwhile, The Wall Street Journal reported that before his ouster, Huang had belittled the executive slated to replace him. Together, these accounts suggest a leadership breakdown that went far beyond a single personal controversy at the top of the firm.

Political pressure and talk of a replacement

The story has also drawn sharp attention in political circles, blurring the line between Silicon Valley boardrooms and Washington commentary. After the resignation became public, Forbes reported that Trump said Huang was "going through hell" following the Nvidia departure, keeping the political spotlight firmly fixed on the company's leadership crisis.

Forbes also noted that Trump again touted Tim Cook, the chief executive of Apple, as a possible replacement at Nvidia. Whether that suggestion carries weight with Nvidia's board remains an open question, yet it gives talk show hosts and market commentators plenty to dissect.
