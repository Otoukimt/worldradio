For broadcasters

LiveRadio is a free directory that helps listeners find radio stations that are already broadcasting publicly on the internet. This page explains how stations appear here and how a broadcaster can change or remove a listing.

We do not host or rebroadcast audio

LiveRadio never stores, records, relays or re-encodes any audio. When a listener presses play, their own browser connects directly to the stream address published by the station, on the station's own server or its streaming provider. The station keeps full control of its stream at all times: if a stream is changed, restricted by region or switched off, it changes here immediately too.

Where the listings come from

Station names, logos and stream addresses come from Radio Browser, an open, community-maintained database of public radio streams. We show each station under its own name and logo and, where available, link to its official website. We do not claim any partnership with or endorsement by the stations listed.

Removal or correction within 48 hours

If you own or manage a station and do not want it listed, or if its name, logo or stream is wrong, write to us from an address connected to the station. Include the station name, its country and, if possible, a link to its page on our site. We remove or correct the listing within 48 hours, and a removed station is blocked so it does not reappear when the directory updates.

contact@worldliveradio.com

Sports and restricted content

Some broadcasts, especially live sport, are covered by rights agreements that limit where they may be streamed. If your agreements require a station or a stream to be unavailable through directories like ours, tell us and we will remove it.

Adding or promoting your station

To add a station, submit it to Radio Browser; it will usually appear here after the next update. If you would like a station page or article on LiveRadio, contact us at the same address.
