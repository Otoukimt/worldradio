1. What LiveRadio is

LiveRadio is a free directory that helps you find radio stations that already broadcast publicly on the internet, together with original articles about radio around the world. It does not host, record, relay or rebroadcast any audio: when you press play, your browser connects directly to the station's own server.

2. Stations and their content

Every station, its programmes, its name and its logo belong to the broadcaster that runs it. We do not choose or control what stations broadcast, and their content does not represent our views. Station information comes from Radio Browser, an open community database.

Streams can stop, change address or be limited to certain countries at any moment, by decision of the broadcaster. We remove stations that do not work, but we cannot guarantee that any particular station will be available.
